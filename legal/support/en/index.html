Avail Support

We reply to messages within 24–48 hours.

1. How to reach us

Write to info@avail.asia. Use your own language — we will understand. To get a faster answer, include the email you signed in with, and the listing title if your question is about a listing.

2. Frequently asked questions

Posting listings is free. You only pay for promotion: bump, highlight and turbo.

Credits are the in-app currency used for promotion. You can buy them in the app: Profile → Wallet → Top up. Credits cannot be exchanged back for money.

You can delete your account yourself: Profile → Delete account. Your listings and chats are removed with it, and unused credits are forfeited.

If notifications do not arrive, check the notification permission in your phone settings and under Profile → Settings.

If a listing was rejected by moderation, the reason arrives in a notification. Fix it and submit the listing again.
