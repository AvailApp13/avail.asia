This rule does not limit the rights of a consumer where the law of the consumer's country of residence requires that the text be provided in the consumer's language.

2. Key points

2.1. Posting listings on Avail is free of charge and unlimited in number. The only paid feature is the promotion of listings using in-app credits, which are purchased through the App Store or Google Play.

2.2. We do not sell your personal data.

2.3. Data is stored on servers in Singapore and is processed by our service providers, including in the United States (Section 10).

2.4. Some features are powered by artificial intelligence (AI): translation, smart and voice search, recognition of price lists, and review of listings. For this purpose, texts are transmitted to AI service providers (Section 5).

2.5. A seller's phone number is visible only to signed-in users, and only via the "Call" button.

2.6. We send you marketing messages from Avail only with your consent. You may opt out at any time.

2.7. You can delete your account within the application itself (Section 12).

3. What data we obtain

3.1. Account and sign-in. Email address, name and sign-in method. You can sign in with email, with Apple, Google or LINE, or by other methods available in the application. When you sign in through a third-party service, we receive from it an identifier and the data you have allowed it to share (for example, your name, email address and profile photo). Processing by that service is governed by its own terms.

3.2. Profile. Name, profile photo, country and city, phone number, application language and registration date.

3.3. Listings. Title, description, photos, price, category, address and map location, terms (for example, the route and stops for tours), contact details, and listing statistics (views and "Call" taps).

3.4. Chats. Messages and photos, the time they were sent and read, and translations of messages.

3.5. Calls and video viewing. Voice and video are transmitted over the internet in real time and are not recorded. We keep a call log: who called whom and in which chat, the start and end time, and the status (answered, declined, missed); the log is visible to the call participants in the chat. A snapshot taken during a video viewing remains only on the phone of the person who took it. To deliver incoming calls, we store a technical device token.

3.6. Support requests. Correspondence in the "Avail Support" chat, attached files and photos, voice messages where this feature is enabled, and the history and outcome of requests.

3.7. Geolocation. With your permission, for nearby search, the "I'm here" button and pinning listings to the map. You can withdraw this permission in your phone settings.

3.8. Voice. When you use voice search or fill in a listing by voice, your speech is recognised by your device's speech recognition service; we do not receive or store the audio recording. What is transmitted to our server is the recognised text, the language, the selected city and, if you have allowed geolocation, your approximate coordinates (Section 5).

3.9. Search and browsing. Search queries and filters, viewed listings, favorites and the selected city.

3.10. Reviews, reports and blocking. Reviews and ratings that you have left or received; reports that you have submitted or that have been submitted about you; the list of users you have blocked; and moderation decisions.

3.11. Wallet and purchases. Credit balance, history of credits and debits (purchases, bonuses, promotion), and purchase details from the App Store or Google Play (item, date, receipt identifier). We do not receive or store bank card details; payments are processed by Apple and Google.

3.12. Device and technical data. Device model, operating system version, language, time zone, application version, a random installation identifier, push notification token, technical logs and crash reports, and information about app launches and sessions.

3.13. Without registration (guests). You can browse listings and use smart search without an account. The application creates a random installation identifier and records usage events without a name or email address. To limit the frequency of a guest's smart search requests, we store the IP address only as an irreversible hash. If you sign in later, these events may be linked to your account.

3.14. The avail.asia website and download links. When you open a link to download the application, we record the date, device type, traffic source (for example, an ad or a social network) and the advertising tag in the link. The IP address and browser details are kept for up to 7 days to match the visit with an ad. The website and the web version use your browser's local storage to remember your language and sign-in.

3.15. Personal Avail link. If you came via a personal Avail link, the application may obtain it from the Google Play install data or from the clipboard (only after you tap "Paste") in order to transfer to your account the listings prepared for you.

3.16. "We'll post for you". If you ask us to post listings on your behalf, we receive what you send us yourself: a price list, catalog, file (Excel, PDF, CSV and others) or a link to your website or cloud drive, as well as your contact details. If you send a link to your website, we take from it only information about your offers. We do not collect listings from websites on our own initiative, only from materials you have sent us.

3.17. Information from others. Other users may provide information about you in a report, review or correspondence. We obtain information about companies from public sources (Section 15).

3.18. Verification documents. If we ask you to confirm your identity, your rights to a property, or a licence or permit (clause 11.4 of the Terms of Use), you send a copy of the document to the "Avail Support" chat. We use it only for that verification. Before sending, mask on the copy any information about religion, blood type and other data not needed for the verification. If the document still contains such information, by sending it you give your explicit consent to its processing for the purpose of the verification. We keep the copy no longer than necessary for the verification, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

4. Why we use data and on what legal basis

4.1. To operate the application (performance of the contract, i.e. the Terms of Use): account and sign-in; publishing, translating and displaying listings; the map; chats, calls and video viewing; reviews; wallet, purchases and promotion; "We'll post for you"; support.

To keep the platform safe (our legitimate interest): review of listings, including automated review using AI; handling reports; blocking; protection against fraud, spam, fake accounts and abuse of bonuses; verification of documents on request (clause 3.18); request rate limiting; protection of our rights in disputes.

4.3. To improve Avail (our legitimate interest): usage statistics, error analysis, improvement of search and AI suggestions, and understanding where users come from.

4.4. Service messages (performance of the contract and legitimate interest): notifications about messages, calls, moderation, balance, security and changes to documents, and welcome messages in the "Avail Support" chat.

4.5. With your consent: geolocation and microphone (phone permissions); marketing messages from Avail (Section 7); transfer of the advertising identifier and hashed contact details to advertising platforms on iPhone (Section 8); and other cases where the law of your country requires consent, including for users from the Russian Federation (Section 17).

4.6. As required by law: responding to lawful requests from public authorities, accounting and tax records, and compliance with court decisions.

4.7. Where the legal basis is your consent, you may withdraw it at any time (Section 13). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

4.8. On Android and in the web version, information for advertising analytics (Section 8) is transmitted on the basis of our legitimate interest in measuring the effectiveness of Avail's advertising. You have the right to object (clause 8.4), and following your objection we will stop the transfer.

5. Artificial intelligence and automated processing

5.1. Translation of listings. The title of a listing is automatically translated into the 8 languages of the application so that users can see it in their own language in the feed and in search. The description is translated when the "Translate" button on the listing page is tapped; the completed translation is saved and shown to subsequent readers.

5.2. Translation in chats. A "Show translation" button appears under a message written in another language. So that the translation appears immediately, after a message is sent its text is transmitted to an AI service provider and translated in advance into the other party's language, even if the button is never tapped. Photos, links, emoji and numbers are not translated.

5.3. Smart and voice search, assistant. The text of the query (typed or recognised from speech), the language, the city and approximate coordinates are transmitted to an AI service provider in order to understand the query and find matching listings. To answer questions about the weather, approximate coordinates are transmitted to a weather service; to search for places, the text of the query is transmitted to a mapping service.

5.4. Recognition of materials. Price lists, catalogs, files, business cards and voice descriptions of listings are processed by AI in order to fill in listing fields. You check the result yourself before publication.

5.5. Review of listings. When listings are published or edited, they are automatically checked for compliance with the Terms of Use, including with the help of AI. Depending on the result, a listing may be published, sent for manual review or rejected.

5.6. AI suggestions. Published listing texts, and the corrections that sellers make to texts suggested by AI, may be used as examples so that AI suggestions in Avail become more accurate.

5.7. Assistance to staff. AI may help our staff prepare replies and analyse technical errors.

5.8. Accuracy. Translations, recognition results, assistant answers and AI suggestions may contain errors and are provided for reference only. Verify the terms of any transaction against the original and confirm them with the other party.

5.9. Moderation decisions. If you disagree with a decision concerning your listing or account, including a decision made automatically, write to the "Avail Support" chat. An appeal is reviewed by a staff member within 7 days.

6. What other users can see and what is published

6.1. The following are visible to all users, including guests: your name, profile photo, city, rating, reviews about you, registration date and your active listings.

6.2. Listings are displayed in the application, in the web version and on the avail.asia website, may be indexed by search engines, and may be used in Avail's advertising and social media (see the Terms of Use for details). Sellers' phone numbers and email addresses are not published on the website. You may object to the use of your listing in advertising by writing to partners@getavail.asia.

6.3. The location of a listing is shown on the map. If there is no exact address, the location is shown approximately, within the district.

6.4. By posting a listing, you agree that signed-in users may obtain your phone number via the "Call" button. To protect phone numbers from bulk harvesting, the number of times a number can be revealed is limited, and the reveal log is kept for 30 days and is visible only to us.

6.5. Chat messages are visible to the other party in the chat. Photos from chats are not published and are accessible only to the chat participants.

7. Messages from Avail

7.1. Service messages. Push notifications, emails and messages in the "Avail Support" chat about your account, messages, calls, listings, moderation, balance, security and changes to documents. You can turn off push notifications in the application settings or your phone settings; we may continue to send important account and security messages after that.

7.2. Welcome messages. After registering, you receive welcome messages in the "Avail Support" chat, including an offer to post your listings for you free of charge based on a price list, catalog, file or link. If you have not read them, we may send you one reminder by push notification.

7.3. Customer relations. After you register, your account is entered into our internal customer relationship management system (CRM), so that staff can see your requests and the source you came from, and can help you with posting listings.

7.4. Marketing messages (promotions, new features, partner offers) are sent only with your consent, for example, if you reply "yes" in the "Avail Support" chat. We try not to contact you too often or at night in your local time.

7.5. Opting out. You may opt out of marketing messages at any time by replying STOP in the "Avail Support" chat or by writing to partners@getavail.asia. Opting out applies across all channels. Turning off notifications in the settings stops push notifications only.

8. Avail advertising and advertising analytics

8.1. We advertise Avail on social networks and video platforms. To understand which ads bring users, the application and our server transmit to these advertising platforms information about events (a tap on a download link, installation, app launch, registration, sign-in), as well as the device model, operating system version and the device's advertising identifier.

8.2. Email addresses and phone numbers are transmitted only as an irreversible hash, and the platform uses them solely to match them with its own users.

8.3. iPhone. The advertising identifier and the hashed email address and phone number are transmitted only if you tapped "Allow" in Apple's tracking prompt. If you did not allow tracking, only the fact of registration is transmitted to the platforms, together with an irreversible hash of your internal account number, without your email address, phone number or advertising identifier. You can change your choice in your iPhone settings.

8.4. Android and the web version. There is no tracking prompt here, and the hashed email address and phone number are transmitted for ad matching (legal basis: clause 4.8). You can reset or disable the advertising identifier in your device settings. To have us stop transmitting hashes of your contact details, write to partners@getavail.asia. We will stop the transfer within 30 days.

8.5. Opting out does not restrict the functioning of the application.

11. How long we keep data

11.1. General rule: account data, listings, chats and support requests are kept for as long as the account exists, and are then deleted or anonymised (Section 12).

11.2. Specific retention periods:
- phone number reveal log: 30 days;
- IP address and browser details when a download link is followed: 7 days;
- text of voice and typed queries to the assistant: 30 days, up to 200 characters, not linked to an account; phone numbers, email addresses and links in the text are masked;
- "We'll post for you" drafts prepared for you that you have not published: 30 days;
- copies of verification documents (clause 3.18): no longer than necessary for the verification;
- the record of your opt-out from marketing messages: indefinitely, so that we do not contact you again;
- information about companies obtained from maps: in accordance with Section 15.

11.3. Technical logs, usage statistics and search history are kept for as long as necessary for security and improvement of the service; after the account is deleted, they are retained only without any link to it.

11.4. We may keep certain data for longer where required by law (for example, payment records) or where it is needed to resolve a dispute, investigate a violation or protect our rights, and only for the period necessary for that purpose.

12. Account deletion

12.1. You can delete your account in the application: Profile → Delete account, confirming with a code sent by email (accounts without a regular email address confirm by typing a word).

12.2. The account and listings are hidden immediately and permanently deleted, together with their photos, after 30 days. If you sign in to the application within those 30 days, the deletion is cancelled.

12.3. Unused credits, including purchased credits, are forfeited upon deletion of the account and are not refunded. We recommend using any remaining credits before deleting your account.

12.4. Messages, photos and reviews that you have sent or left for other users remain with them, but without your name or photo, marked "Account deleted".

12.5. You can also delete your account or ask a question about deletion by writing to partners@getavail.asia.

13. Your rights

13.1. You have the right to:
- find out what data about you we process and obtain a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate data (most of it yourself, in your profile);
- delete your data or your account;
- restrict processing or object to it, including processing based on our legitimate interest;
- receive your data in a format suitable for transfer, where provided for by law;
- withdraw your consent at any time;
- ask for a moderation decision, including an automated one, to be reviewed by a staff member (clause 5.9);
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Thailand, with the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); in other countries, with the authority of your country (Sections 17 and 18).

13.2. How to exercise your rights: write to partners@getavail.asia from the email address you use to sign in, or write to the "Avail Support" chat. We may ask you to confirm that you are the account holder.

13.3. We respond within 30 days, unless the law of your country sets a shorter period.

13.4. We may refuse a request or fulfil it in part where permitted by law (for example, if a request is manifestly unfounded or affects the rights of other people). We will tell you the reason for the refusal.

13.5. Declining to provide certain data may limit the functioning of features: for example, nearby search does not work without geolocation, and without a phone number buyers will not be able to call you. An email address or another sign-in method is required to enter into and perform the contract: registration is not possible without it. All other data is provided at your discretion.

14. Security and data breaches

14.1. We apply reasonable organisational and technical safeguards: encryption in transit, database-level access control, private storage of photos and files from chats, revealing phone numbers one at a time, and restricted staff access.

14.2. No system is completely secure. Do not share passwords or codes from emails with anyone, including people who claim to be Avail staff.

14.3. If a data breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the supervisory authority within the time limits and in the manner established by law, and will notify you without undue delay where the law so requires.
