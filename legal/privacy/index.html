Privacy Policy of the Avail mobile application

Version 6. Effective date: October 30, 2026.

1. Introduction

Avail ("platform", "we") respects your privacy. This Policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with and what rights you have. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. Data we collect

- Account data: name, email address, phone number, country and city, profile photo. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an identifier and the data you allowed to be shared.

- Content: listings and their photos, reviews, chat messages, support requests.

- Geolocation: with your permission — for nearby search and pinning listings to the map. Listing coordinates are published on the map; if no exact address is given, the point is shown approximately (within the district).

- Voice: when you use voice search or post a listing by voice, your speech is recognised by your device's speech recognition service (Apple or Google) and the recording is not stored. The recognised text is processed by AI to fill in the listing fields or run the search.

- In-app calls: during an audio call between users, the voice is transmitted over the internet via a third-party real-time communication service. Calls are not recorded or stored. We keep a call log — who called whom and in which chat, start and end time, and status (answered, declined, missed); it is shown to the call participants in the chat. To deliver incoming calls we store a technical device token. Video viewing works the same way: at the buyer's request, the seller shows the listing on video; the video is not recorded or stored, and a snapshot stays only on the phone of the person who took it.

- Device data: model, operating system version, language, device identifiers, push notification token.

- Usage data: views and searches, "Call" taps on listings (statistics for the seller), technical error logs.

- Payments: information about purchases via Apple App Store / Google Play (the fact and contents of the purchase). We never receive or store your card details.

3. How we use data

- Operating the service: account, publishing and displaying listings, chats and calls, reviews, paid services and internal balance.

- Safety and moderation: checking listings (including automated, AI-assisted checks), handling reports, fraud prevention.

- Notifications: push notifications about messages, moderation and events; they can be disabled in settings.

- Service improvement: analysis of usage and errors.

4. What other users see

Your name, profile photo, city, rating, reviews, registration date and active listings are visible to all users of the app. By posting a listing you agree that your phone number is available to users for contact via the "Call" button. Chat messages are visible to your interlocutor.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only to the extent necessary for the service to work, with the following categories of recipients:

- cloud infrastructure providers — database and file hosting;
- push notification delivery services;
- Apple / Google — when processing in-app purchases and recognising speech;
- a real-time communication service — transmitting voice during in-app calls;
- third-party automated services, including AI — message translation, recognition of listing text during bulk upload, smart search, moderation;
- geocoding and map services — determining coordinates from listing addresses and displaying maps.

All recipients process data on our behalf and only for the purposes of operating the service. Data may be disclosed to law enforcement and other authorised bodies in cases provided by law. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention

Data is stored while your account is active. After account deletion data is deleted or anonymised; certain records (payment history, violation records) may be kept longer where required by law or to protect our rights. Messages and reviews you left for other users stay with them without your name or photo (marked “Account deleted”).

7. Business contacts (CRM)

This section is not about app users but about companies and their staff whom Avail invites to post listings: rental shops, property owners, tour operators and other businesses.

- What data: company name, type of business, district and address, website; business contacts — email, phone, WhatsApp, LINE, social media pages; the contact person's name and position, if published; the history of our communication (emails, messages, calls and their outcome); whether the company has registered in Avail and posted listings.

- Where from: public sources the company published itself — its website and pages, maps and directories; a business card handed to us; the company itself. Information from Google Maps is used only as a hint: if the company has not confirmed it, we delete it after 30 days and never share it.

- Why: to invite the company to post listings on Avail for free, help with registration and uploading, grant a bonus and answer questions. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in business communication; if you gave us your business card or contacted us yourself, your consent.

- How we contact you: emails from hello@avail.asia — without a reply from you, no more than two are sent automatically; WhatsApp and LINE messages sent manually by our staff; phone calls.

- Opting out: every email contains an "Unsubscribe" link; in a messenger, just reply STOP; you can also write to info@avail.asia. After you opt out we no longer send you any marketing messages on any channel. To honour this we keep only an encrypted opt-out record.

- Who receives the data: the email service that sends our emails; a mailing service; cloud infrastructure; AI services — to translate correspondence and read business cards. Data may be processed outside Thailand with appropriate safeguards. We do not sell contacts or share them with others for their advertising.

- How long we keep it: while communication is ongoing; if the company declined or did not reply, we anonymise the data after 18 months. The opt-out record is kept indefinitely so that we never write to you again.

- Link to an account: if the company or its employee registers in Avail, we link the business record to the account to grant the bonus and understand where the user came from.

- Your rights: to find out what data we hold about you, to correct it, delete it or object to its processing — write to info@avail.asia; we reply within 30 days.

8. Your rights

- Edit your profile data in the app.
- Delete your account yourself: Profile → Delete account, confirmed with a code from an email (accounts without a regular email confirm with a word). The account and your listings are hidden at once and permanently deleted 30 days later together with their photos; signing in within those 30 days cancels the deletion. Any unused internal balance is forfeited.
- Disable notifications in settings; revoke geolocation and microphone permissions in your device settings.
- Request access to, correction or deletion of your data at info@avail.asia.

9. Security

We apply reasonable organisational and technical safeguards: encryption in transit, database-level access control, restricted staff access.

10. Minors

The app is intended for persons over 18. We do not knowingly collect data of minors; such accounts are subject to deletion.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. A new version takes effect upon publication in the app; material changes will be additionally announced. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: info@avail.asia.
